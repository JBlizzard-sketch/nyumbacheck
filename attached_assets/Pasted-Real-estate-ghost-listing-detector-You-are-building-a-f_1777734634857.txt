Real estate ghost listing detector


You are building a full production-grade property listing fraud detection and market intelligence platform for the Nairobi real estate market. Read the full brief, expand the vision, present ~20 phased plan, wait for "next."
The pain:
The Nairobi property market — both rental and sale — is riddled with fake, duplicate, and ghost listings. A flat in Kilimani is listed by four different agents at four different prices, three of which are already taken. Buyers and tenants waste days or weeks chasing listings that don't exist, visiting properties that have already gone, or getting scammed by agents collecting "viewing fees" for properties they don't control. BuyRentKenya, JumiaHouses, and Facebook Marketplace are full of this. There is no service that audits this, flags it, or helps either landlords or serious buyers navigate it with data.
What you are building:
A platform with two products in one. First, a fraud detection report — a landlord or serious buyer submits a listing URL or property address and within hours receives a report showing: how many times this property appears across platforms, price inconsistencies, agent legitimacy check, days-on-market anomalies, and a fraud risk score. Second, a market intelligence subscription — serious buyers, property investors, and real estate agents pay a monthly fee for access to clean, deduplicated market data: real price per sqft by neighbourhood, actual days-on-market, listing velocity, and neighbourhood price trend charts. This is data that currently does not exist cleanly anywhere in Kenya.
Critical nuances:
Scraping Kenyan property sites must be done carefully — build a respectful scraper with rate limiting and proper caching so you don't get blocked. Duplicate detection should use image hashing (the same flat photo appearing on multiple listings), address normalisation (different spellings of the same street), and price clustering. The fraud score must be explainable — "this listing appears on 4 platforms at prices ranging from Ksh 45k to Ksh 80k, and the agent phone number matches 23 other listings" — not a black box. The market intelligence product is the long-term revenue driver — agents and investors pay for data subscriptions. The report product is the acquisition funnel.
Expand this. Think about: a browser extension that shows a fraud score overlay when you're browsing BuyRentKenya; an agent reputation score built over time from listing quality data; a "verified listing" badge programme where landlords pay to have their listing certified clean; email alerts when a saved property drops in price or a new listing matching your criteria appears; and a data API for proptech developers building on top of your dataset.
Hosting: Scraping and data pipeline as a containerised Python backend on Railway (or Docker/VPS for heavier scraping workloads). Next.js frontend on Vercel. PostgreSQL for listings database. Background job scheduler for daily scraping runs. Docker-compose for the full local dev environment.
